# Hestia — Role-based training plan: Acme ERP Transformation Pilot
Curriculum draft only; final training must reflect the client's actual configured system.

## Learning tracks
- End users: perform daily transactions and resolve common errors
- Managers: approve exceptions and review KPI dashboards
- Finance: control periods, reconcile balances and handle reporting
- IT administrators: permissions, integrations, escalation and audit logging

## Hands-on exercises linked to actual workflows
- Demonstrate quote-to-cash; process a valid example, then an exception, then a role-permission check
- Demonstrate purchase-to-pay; process a valid example, then an exception, then a role-permission check
- Demonstrate month-end close; process a valid example, then an exception, then a role-permission check
- Demonstrate inventory receiving; process a valid example, then an exception, then a role-permission check

## Sign-off evidence
- Training attendance and role assignment
- Practice transactions completed in sandbox
- Accuracy check of user-facing job aids
- Human trainer/manager sign-off and support contacts

Do not use live customer or employee data in training without authorization.
