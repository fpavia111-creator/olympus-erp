# Olympus go-to-market: measurable pilot before big claims

## Primary customer

Small ERP implementation partners and midmarket finance/operations teams using CSV and Excel exports to prepare migration into ERP, CRM or HCM targets. Good initial jobs: master data cleanup, open AR/AP, inventory quantities, referential integrity, and journal reconciliation.

## Packaging experiments (NOT validated willingness-to-pay)

1. **Free local readiness preview**: customer runs a local demo on synthetic data, or an anonymized schema-only walkthrough. No sensitive upload required.
2. **Hermes Partner Seat**: test **$99-$299/month** per consultant. Includes local scan, export, detailed report. Verify seat management, license terms and support scope before charging.
3. **Fixed-scope Migration Readiness Scan**: test **$1,500-$5,000** per project. Deliver evidence-backed report, rejection queue, explicit mapping review, client-owned clean exports, and a human debrief. Define data size, object count, rounds and reconciliation terms in contract.
4. **Later**: white-label reporting, secure hosted scans, paid implementation, expanded integrations, Themis enterprise identity features.

**Current direction (Oct 2026):** lead with the Hermes + Poseidon **Migration Integrity Scan**: a scoped readiness review ($499–$1,500) and a trial-load assurance project ($1,500–$5,000) for NetSuite consultants and mid-market teams, on top of the free MIT tool and demo. The bands above are older proposals, not verified market prices. Do not market the historical hours or ROI estimates as actual savings without controlled pilot measurement.

## Pilot success criteria

Recruit one friendly business or implementation partner. Under NDA/DPA as applicable, run a **local-only** pilot with a representative export dataset. Baseline the time needed to clean/reconcile manually and compare the same scope against the Hermes-assisted process. Record sample size, rows, field coverage, minutes spent, reviewer interventions, missed defects, false alarms, and sandbox-import outcome.

Success is **measured**, not asserted. Track at least: line-level detection precision, false positives, number of required manual mappings, sandbox acceptance rate, hours actually saved, and end-user trust. Compare the estimated hours against actual logged time and revise weights.

## Outreach sequence

1. Peitho scores only lead facts that were independently reviewed by a human, including a public source note and an explicit suppression field.
2. Introduce *a short call and representative report*, never a demand for production records.
3. Ask about source system, target tenant, number of objects, data volumes, privacy obligations, and who approves field mappings.
4. Quote a fixed-scope paid pilot only after reviewing object inventory and data constraints.
5. Collect testimonial, anonymized case study and factual KPI results only with written consent.

## Buyer objection responses

**"Our ERP partner already does this."** Hermes is a pre-migration triage tool for their team, not a substitute for the partner's configuration, training and cutover services.

**"Can it guarantee our import?"** No. Every target tenant can have unique required fields, permission settings and API/import constraints. A sandbox import remains mandatory.

**"We can't upload customer or salary data."** The current CLI runs on the customer's own machine without network transmission. Use a scoped contractor agreement and approved handling process for any paid assisted service.

**"How accurate is your hours estimate?"** It is a planning heuristic. Pilot data should be collected before making an accuracy claim.

## 30-day practical progression

- Days 1-7: verify 11 initial templates with 2-3 real export schemas (anonymized). Find mis-mappings and test against tenant sandbox.
- Days 8-14: recruit two potential pilot buyers, document data processing and deliverable acceptance checklist.
- Days 15-21: run the first controlled pilot and measure outcomes, capture feedback and revise mappings.
- Days 22-30: test one paid readiness scan offer, decide on target niche, and decide whether hosted deployment is justified.

## Where AI fits

AI can suggest mappings, explain reasons for errors and draft outreach, **but not silently approve data decisions, sign legal attestations or claim completed migration**. Add human checkpoints at mapping approval, rejected-data resolution and final sandbox reconciliation.
