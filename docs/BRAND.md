# Brand architecture: The Olympus Suite

| Previous internal name | Greek name | Mythological inspiration | Real product job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LoadReady | **Hermes** | Messenger who guides journeys | Carries legacy data toward its new system safely; checks, maps, cleans and reports |
| Closer | **Peitho** | Personification of persuasion | Ethical, evidence-led sales pipeline and outreach drafting |
| — | **Themis** | Goddess associated with justice, law and orderly procedure | Mapping-spec sign-off: explicit human approval bound to the spec's SHA-256, invalidated on change |
| Shared umbrella | **Olympus** | Home of the gods | Shared identity and future product integrations |

Module names inside the one Olympus workspace: **Athena** (discovery), **Poseidon** (trial-load reconciliation), **Apollo/Clio** (UAT steps and evidence), **Chronos** (cutover), **Zeus** (conservative go-live gate), **Argus** (local ERP hypercare incident queue), **Hestia** (training), **Plutus** (ROI scenarios), **Daedalus** (integration design). These are module names, not separate products or autonomous AI agents.

**Argus naming note:** Olympus Argus (ERP hypercare incident queue) is unrelated to Kurt Francisco's separate product *Argus of MCP* (MCP server and skill scanner). Always say "Olympus Argus" in public copy where both could appear.

## Core story

- "**Know what will fail before the migration starts.**"
- Hermes is *local-first*, produces *line-numbered findings*, and makes uncertainty visible.
- Themis prevents silent specification changes after a human approves the mapping.
- Peitho begins with verifiable business context; never claim a company is migrating without checking.

Visual system suggestion: midnight navy (#132239), antique gold (#EABF79), turquoise (#107D85), cream (#F8F6F0). Use classical influence, not a cluttered fantasy theme. Plain enterprise terminology should remain more prominent than mythology in product interfaces.

**Names are provisional** pending trademark and domain clearance. Do not imply that the software is affiliated with a mythology publisher, a vendor, or the company named in the uploaded business analysis.
